## Measuring forecast usefulness

Retain publication and receipt times, original text, source ID and screening
version. Evaluate all eligible posts, including uncertain and unrelated controls.
Do not infer direction automatically from popularity. A future explicit-call
evaluation needs the stated asset, direction, horizon, invalidation and a rule
for ambiguous or withdrawn calls, all recorded before the result is known.

The current one-, five-, fifteen- and sixty-minute candle studies can measure
immediate price response. They cannot validate a multi-week chart forecast,
establish causality, or rank these accounts by profitable trading performance.
Repeated reports and posts by the same data provider are correlated evidence.
